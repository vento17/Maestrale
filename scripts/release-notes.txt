0.4.0 — LiveForge is now Maestrale: ETC Eos surface, Shell / Palette / Merge,
connections that come back, Ctrl+Z everywhere

- New name and new logo: LiveForge is Maestrale. Same program — projects saved
  before the rename (.liveforge) open exactly as they are; on Windows the
  portable build is now Maestrale.exe, in ../MaestraleExe
- ETC widget: an Eos Gio @5 surface laid out like the desk. Every key sends
  /eos/key/<name>; four encoders that page with the desk from Eos's own wheel
  list, each with its own speed (Intensity x0.1, Pan/Tilt x20, Zoom x100, and
  "Save as default" to keep yours); the vertical level wheel; the two cue
  masters and a Grandmaster fader; GO and STOP as inputs, so a cue or a Clock
  row can press them
- Eos user, per widget: everything goes out as /eos/user/<n>/... so Maestrale
  has its own selection and command line and does not fight whoever is at the
  console — on the ETC and Encoder widgets, and on any widget sending to /eos/
- Encoder widget: endless encoders that send only the change, never a value,
  so the desk never jumps. Presets for Eos, grandMA3 and MIDI relative
- Encoders at the screen edge: with a mouse the pointer is locked, so a drag
  never runs out of room; with a finger, hold still and the encoder keeps
  turning (upper/right half +, lower/left −), slide to speed up or turn back
- Shell widget: buttons that act on the PC — turn off, restart, sleep,
  hibernate, lock, screens off, close or restart Maestrale, open a file,
  folder or web page, close a program, any command line. Turn off and restart
  count down first and save where Maestrale is; each button is an input, so a
  Clock row turns the PC off at 23:00
- Palette widget: colour swatches, tap to pick, hold or right-click for a
  colour wheel with RGB / CMY / HSV sliders. Every colour puts out R, G, B
- Merge widget: drop as many cables as you like on one box; merges them in
  groups (3 = R G B) HTP, LTP, lowest or average, with a smooth glide.
  Palette → Merge (LTP, by 3, 0.5 s) → three faders = colour presets
- Presets for the cell banks: Lights DMX (RGB, RGBW, moving head...),
  ETC Eos (submasters, faders, channels, pan/tilt, macros, cues, GO/STOP,
  channel and group select) and TouchDesigner (mixer, post FX, camera);
  make, edit and delete your own, with a pattern that fills the addresses
- XY Pad presets: Eos channel / group / selection pan-tilt and XYZ focus,
  OSC /xyN, TouchDesigner cameras and lights, DMX moving head, MIDI CC.
  Optional Z slider on the right edge, starting in the middle
- Sketch: drawing presets (circle, spiral, star, Lissajous, random walk...),
  Shift-drag to move a line and Ctrl-drag to scale it, and play backwards
- Cues store what the generators are doing (sketch lines, LFO and noise
  settings, step sequencer steps, graph points); exclude lists for cues and
  submasters
- Router outputs: smooth and spring, each a switch and a slider, with the
  smooth running on the way up, both ways or on the way down
- Router page: Tab walks down the column, link after link, or across the row
- Installations: ports, IPs and which protocols were connected go into the
  project file at once, so it reopens connected exactly as it was left, even
  after a crash or a power cut; Settings has "Start Maestrale with Windows",
  and Live mode at startup is on by default
- Ctrl+Z everywhere, in Live as well as in Edit: a line drawn or cleared on a
  Sketch, a cue, a submaster scene, a colour, a widget moved. 50 steps back
- Enter in any field takes the value and leaves it — no more reaching for the
  mouse after typing a number
- Shift patching: a tap on Shift switches patching on and leaves it on (one
  drag, one cable); holding Shift keeps the cable on the pointer for many
  drops from one output; another tap saves everything at once
- In Live, touching a widget anywhere — a control, its own bar, an empty
  corner — shows its parameters on the right; the right-hand column can be
  dragged wider and double-clicked back to normal
- More in the Live right bar: Merge (mode, inputs per group, smooth), Sketch
  (drawing preset, seconds per run, speed, loop, dots, delay, smooth) and
  Palette (how many colours, per row, slider type)
- Clock: a play button on each row fires it now, without waiting for the
  time, and rows fire whatever page is on screen
- Settings: the connection sections are outlined in the interface colour
