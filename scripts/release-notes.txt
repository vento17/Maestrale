0.4.3 - Live keeps its state, parameter tabs, Startup widget, a delay on every Shell button

- Going to Edit and back no longer resets Live. Live used to be torn down
  when you left it, so every LFO phase, timeline position and running sound
  started over when you came back. It now stays mounted behind the editor:
  the show keeps running while you edit, and everything is where you left it
- Parameter tabs: a widget's parameters are split into Widget / Look / Output,
  the same three on every widget, in Edit and in Live. No more scrolling past
  position and colours to reach a setting. The tab you pick is remembered
  from widget to widget; Live gets a Look tab of its own (name, title font,
  edge, font size)
- New Startup widget: fires triggers when the show starts, each one after
  its own delay. Cable them with Shift to a cue's GO, a sound, a Shell
  button, a timeline PLAY, or give a trigger its own OSC / MIDI / DMX output.
  Once per run of Maestrale by default, or every time Live opens
- Shell: every button can wait a delay before it runs, not only turn off and
  restart. It counts down on the widget, with Cancel
- Submasters: recording a scene puts its fader at full, so the look you just
  stored stays up through it
- Shift patching: the widgets keep their own look instead of going blank.
  Every dot says what it is on hover, IN or OUT, including the free ones
- Timeline: every track has its own output dot, on its row
- A row's output dot (cues, sound tracks, timeline tracks, keys) is always on
  its right, however tall the row is
- New Maestrale logo
