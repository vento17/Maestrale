0.4.1 - iControls with layers and buttons, Open DMX fixed, undo that costs nothing

- New iControls widget: an iCON iControls / iControls Pro comes in as four
  pages, one per layer of the surface, each with 9 faders, 9 knobs and 32
  buttons - 200 cells to patch anywhere. The Layer buttons on the unit change
  the page, and so do the arrows in the widget
- Every page carries its own MIDI map, because the layers of a real unit use
  different and unpredictable CC numbers. "Learn" fills a row by moving the
  controls instead of reading numbers out of iMap
- The surface works whatever page of the project is on screen. Its listener
  used to live inside the widget, which React unmounts as soon as you look at
  another page, so a surface built on page 1 went deaf on page 2
- MIDI ports are matched without the index RtMidi puts on the end of their
  names. That index moves when any other MIDI device is plugged or unplugged,
  so a saved choice silently became a different physical port - on a unit with
  two ports, the one that does nothing
- Enttec Open DMX: the FTDI driver now opens the dongle properly
- Open DMX timing: a frame is 513 bytes at 250 kbaud, 22.6 ms on the wire, and
  the loop was sending one every 22 ms - each cycle cut the previous frame in
  half and fixtures flickered. Now 30 ms, with the break and mark-after-break
  held for a measured 120 and 20 microseconds instead of however long a USB
  transfer happened to take
- Moving a fader no longer sends one IPC message per pointer event - hundreds a
  second on a high-polling mouse, which starved the main process where the DMX
  frame timer lives. Changes are folded by channel and sent at most every 15 ms
- Undo keeps a reference to the previous project instead of a deep copy of the
  whole thing. Fifty deep copies of a project carrying a sound file ran the
  renderer out of heap, and pulling a cable or a copy-paste was enough to get
  there. Ten steps are kept, and they cost nothing
- Each cable is saved the moment it is dropped: one link, one undo step. They
  used to be queued and written when Shift was tapped again, so a patch made
  and never confirmed was quietly lost
- Cell editor: a bank set to Enttec Open DMX shows its DMX channel, like
  Art-Net and sACN already did. The column was missing altogether
- Cells added to a bank follow the bank's protocol. Growing a DMX, OSC or sACN
  bank used to hand the new cells MIDI defaults
- Timeline: the Edit preview no longer crashes on a timeline holding a cue,
  wait or sound track - it assumed every track had keyframes

Known: the Sound Player still stores audio inside the project file as base64,
so a project with a long sound in it becomes very large and slow to save.
